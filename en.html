Frequently asked questions

Short and clear

What is Link2Group?

Link2Group is the company behind Link2Leads and Link2Talent. It is based in Amsterdam and was founded by Anne-Roos Mol and Demi Stekelenburg. Link2Leads runs cold email campaigns for B2B companies. Link2Talent provides remote setters and closers.

What does Link2Leads do?

Link2Leads finds companies that look like your best customer, emails them personally on your behalf and forwards only the positive replies to your inbox. Packages range from €995 to €1,995 per month, excluding VAT. The campaign is ready within two weeks and goes live in week 3. See the packages.

What does Link2Talent do?

Link2Talent matches B2B companies with Dutch-speaking remote setters and closers. We have placed more than 100 of them. A setter is active within 14 days, on an hourly rate, performance basis or a fixed price per seat. See the models.

Can I combine Link2Leads and Link2Talent?

Yes. Link2Leads delivers the positive replies and a Link2Talent setter calls them back and books the meeting in your calendar. You discuss the combination in the free Link2Leads fit check.
